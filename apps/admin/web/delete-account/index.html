Delete your PataStay account

Last updated: 3 November 2026

In the app (quickest)

  1. Open PataStay and sign in.
  2. Tap Profile.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete account, then confirm.

You will be signed out straight away. If you are a host, your listings are hidden from guests immediately.

Without the app

Email patastay.help@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my PataStay account". Send it from the email address you signed up with, so we can confirm the account is yours.

What gets deleted

Within 30 days of your request, we permanently delete:

  • your account, name, email address, phone number and password;
  • your listings, photos, contact numbers and exact map locations;
  • your saved places and sign-in sessions.

What we keep

  • Enquiries you sent to hosts: these stay in the host's booking history, but your name, contact details and message are removed. Only the dates and number of guests remain.
  • Reports and listing activity records: kept for safety and fraud prevention, but no longer linked to you.
  • A deletion record: your account ID, the dates of the request and of the deletion, and counts of what was removed, so we can show the deletion was carried out. It contains no name, email or phone number.

Copies in our providers' backups and logs are replaced in their normal cycle. Deletion cannot be undone. If you change your mind before it is processed, email us and we can cancel the request.
