Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026 · Last updated: 3 November 2026

Draft. This policy is being reviewed and may change before PataStay launches publicly.

PataStay is a mobile app for finding short-stay accommodation in Kenya, such as apartments, guest houses and holiday homes. Hosts list places to stay, and guests contact hosts directly. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the rights you have under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.

In short

  • We collect only what is needed to show listings, connect guests with hosts and keep the platform safe.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not show adverts.
  • A host's phone number is shown only to signed-in users who choose to contact that host.
  • A listing's exact address pin stays private. Guests see an approximate location.
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time. We complete the deletion within 30 days.

1. Who we are

PataStay is operated by Leah Gitau in Kenya ("PataStay", "we", "us"). We are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy.

You can contact us about privacy at patastay.help@gmail.com.

2. The data we collect

When you create an account

  • Your name, email address and password. Passwords are stored only in encrypted (hashed) form, so we cannot read them.
  • Your phone number, if you add one.
  • Whether you are a guest, a host or both, and the date you joined.

When you list a property (hosts)

  • Listing details: title, description, property type, rooms, amenities, house rules, nightly price, minimum stay and the dates the property is unavailable.
  • Photos of the property. Do not include people, car number plates or documents in your photos.
  • The property's area and exact map location. The exact pin is visible only to you and to PataStay administrators. Other users see a point moved between 100 and 300 metres from the real location.
  • A contact phone number and, optionally, a WhatsApp number for guests to reach you.
  • Records of the checks we carry out before giving a listing a "Verified" badge, such as an identity check, proof that you own or may let the property, and a visit or video walkthrough. We record each check's result and short notes. If you show us an ID document, we do not keep a copy of it.

When you use the app as a guest

  • Enquiries: the name and contact details you enter, your travel dates, the number of guests, your message and the host's reply.
  • Saved places: the listings you save as favourites.
  • Reports: if you report a listing, the reason and any description you give.
  • Contact requests: when you tap to call or WhatsApp a host, we record that you requested that host's number. This lets us detect misuse, and we limit how many numbers one account can request in an hour.

Collected automatically

  • Listing activity: views, shares, saves, contact taps and enquiries, linked to the listing and, if you are signed in, to your account. This lets hosts see how their listings perform and helps us spot fraud.
  • Your location: only when you use "Near me" and allow location access on your phone. Your location is sent once to find nearby listings and is not stored. You can turn off location access in your phone's settings at any time.
  • Technical records: our hosting providers keep short-lived security and error logs, such as your IP address and the time of a request. Our own app logs record what kind of error happened, not your email, phone number or messages.
  • On your device: the app stores your sign-in session and settings, such as the light or dark theme, on your phone. We do not use advertising trackers or cookies.

4. Who can see your data

  • Everyone, including people who are not signed in: published listings (photos, description, price, approximate location) and the host's first name, the month and year they joined and their number of listings.
  • Signed-in users who choose to contact a host: that host's phone or WhatsApp number.
  • The host you send an enquiry to: the name, contact details, dates and message in your enquiry.
  • PataStay administrators: account and listing data, as needed to review listings, verify hosts, handle reports and help users. Administrator actions are recorded.

When you contact a host by phone or WhatsApp, that conversation happens outside PataStay. WhatsApp is provided by Meta, and its own privacy policy applies.

6. Data stored outside Kenya

Our main database is in the European Union, and some providers operate in other countries. Where data leaves Kenya, we rely on providers that offer appropriate safeguards for personal data, as required by Part VI of the Data Protection Act. The EU's data protection law (GDPR) gives strong protection to data held there.

7. How long we keep data

  • Your account and listings: while your account is open.
  • When you delete your account: your listings are hidden at once. Within 30 days we permanently delete your account, profile, listings, photos, contact numbers, exact location pins, saved places and sign-in sessions.
  • Enquiries you sent: these stay in the host's records as the history of their bookings, but we remove your name, contact details and message. Only the dates and number of guests remain.
  • Reports and activity records: we keep these to protect the platform, but they are no longer linked to your account.
  • Proof of deletion: we keep a short record showing your account ID, the dates of your request and of its deletion, and how many items were removed. It contains no name, email or phone number.
  • Provider backups and logs: these may hold copies for a limited period until they are replaced in the provider's normal cycle.

8. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to:

  • be told how your personal data is used, which is what this policy does;
  • get a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • have inaccurate or out-of-date data corrected. You can edit most of your details in the app;
  • have your data deleted. See Delete your account;
  • object to our using your data, or ask us to restrict how we use it;
  • receive your data in a commonly used, machine-readable format;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, for example by turning off location access.

To use any of these rights, email patastay.help@gmail.com from the email address on your account. We will reply within the time the law allows, usually within 30 days, and we may need to confirm your identity first.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please contact us first. You also have the right to complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) at www.odpc.go.ke.

9. How we protect your data

  • All connections to PataStay are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Database access rules check every request, so you can only read and change what you are allowed to.
  • Phone numbers and exact locations are kept separate from public listing data.
  • Contact-number requests are limited per account, to stop people collecting numbers in bulk.
  • Only a small number of administrators can access the administration website, and their actions are logged.

No system is perfectly secure. If a data breach is likely to put you at risk, we will tell you and the Data Protection Commissioner as the law requires.

10. Children

PataStay is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as PataStay changes. When we make important changes, we will tell you in the app or by email before they take effect. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version.
